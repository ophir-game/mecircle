Identity-gated rotating savings circles (ROSCA) on ME Network

MECircle turns the community savings circle — known across Indonesia as arisan — into a transparent smart-contract system. Members must hold a verified on-chain identity attestation to join, every deposit is recorded in public, and a reputation ledger remembers who pays on time. No treasurer holding the cash, no trust-me bro: the contract holds the pot and pays each round's recipient in turn.

How it works

  1. Get verified. An attestor marks your address as identity-verified in the AttestationRegistry. Unverified wallets cannot join a circle.
  2. Create or join a circle. Pick a contribution (e.g. 0.01 MEC), the number of seats, and the round length. The circle starts once its seats fill.
  3. Deposit every round. Each round, every member deposits the same contribution. The full pot goes to one member — round 1's recipient first, then the next, in join order.
  4. Build reputation. On-time deposits and completed circles are written to the ReputationLedger, so a good history follows your address to the next circle.
